Two Ascend 950 SuperPoD architectures at 10k–500k NPUs: hardware scale, reliability and bottlenecks

Subjects: Huawei’s native Atlas 950 SuperPoD (current product-page spec: 16 compute + 4 UB interconnect cabinets, 1,024 NPUs) and SuperPoD Flex / UBL128 (defined jointly by Huawei and DeepSeek: 128 NPUs on a 3.2 Tbps single-layer UB switch). Hardware counts come from the HAC-D v4.3 engine’s a950-ub2 template (UBL128 = the same template with another SuperPoD geometry, Section 2); reliability is that of one synchronous training job with the six default mechanisms (D-SDC, R-SDC, R-REPLAY, R-PROCESS, R-ELASTIC, C-FAST, Section 5). Both use the same scale-out between SuperPoDs (4 planes × 2 tiers, radix 1,024, 4 × 200G per NPU (main case)); other designs in 3.4. Architecture background: docs/ASCEND_CLUSTER_ARCHITECTURE.zh.html; Section 6 gives the Shapley contributions of the latest ReMAP mechanism set (23).

  • Hardware: compute is the same in both (64-NPU cabinets, 7,813 compute cabinets at 500k); the UB domain differs: native has 4 interconnect cabinets, 16 switches and 1,152 LRS per 1,024 NPUs; UBL128 has 4 switches per 128 NPUs and no LRS but twice the optical links per NPU (4 vs 2). At 500k: 1,954 (native) vs 977 (UBL128) UB network racks.
  • Reliability with scale: A_op 99.92 % (10k) → 99.20 % (100k) → 98.42 % (200k) → 96.15 % (500k, native); MTBF_hw from 32.3 h down to 0.646 h (39 min), MTTR_hw ≈ 88 s almost independent of size. UBL128 is 0.004–0.21 points higher.
  • Thresholds: below 99 % from ≈ 126k (native) / 134k (UBL128) NPUs, below 98.5 % from ≈ 190k / 201k, MTBF_hw under 1 h from ≈ 323k. At 500k, 98.5 % needs FIT per NPU down to 1,068 (today 3,097) or MTTR down to 30 s.
  • Bottleneck: the classes without a fast recovery rung — job-level shared services (storage, unattributed / software), management and board IO, cabinet power / cooling, switches — are 30 % of the hardware interruptions but 89 % of the downtime (264.5 s of global recovery each). The NPU itself is 54 % of the interruptions but only 9 % of the downtime (absorbed by replay and elastic recovery). The bottleneck is the same in both architectures.
  • 口径 and limits: failure rates and mechanism parameters are the values of the one catalog shared by ReMAP and ai-cluster-reliability (54 events, FIT per card, an analogue prior from GPU fleets; all 8 checks pass, see HACD_REMAP_COMPARISON); HAC-D’s topology templates then convert them per hardware unit under the CloudMatrix384 geometry, leaving the 950 about 7 % below the repositories’ arithmetic (their results are the last row of Table 1); none are 950 measurements; UBL128 beyond “128 NPUs, 3.2 Tbps, single-layer switch, two-tier scale-out” is assumed; the 950 scale-out planes, tiers and radix are not public.

3.3 Single points of failure and blast radius (Table 5)
