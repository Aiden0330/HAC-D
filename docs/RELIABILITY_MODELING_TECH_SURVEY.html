Summary. Reliability modeling for AI clusters is a ladder from cheap to expensive methods: 口径-typed failure data, closed forms (RBD, CTMC, checkpoint renewal, MTTR pipelines, elasticity first-order models, silent-corruption latent states, allocation), discrete-event and Monte Carlo simulation, performance simulators used as parameter suppliers, data-driven models that feed the inputs, device-level fault injection for silent corruption, and a calibration and validation loop. At 500k NPUs the job interruption interval is minutes, so the technologies that matter are the ones that move the cluster out of the restart-dominated regime: failure domains that degrade instead of stopping, scale-independent detection, local recovery instead of global restart, loosely coupled training, near-zero exposed checkpoint cost, silent corruption managed as its own budget, spares and repair treated as a queue, fleet hygiene, and an observability loop that keeps the models calibrated.

Inference-specific pieces

  • N+k capacity availability rather than "service up": a replica is a series of TP/EP ranks, the service is a k-of-n of replicas with capacity, and the SLO is evaluated over a window.
  • Request-level DES with failover and cold start. A 183 s vLLM cold start exceeds the 2-minute spot preemption warning, which is why SkyServe over-provisions spot replicas and falls back to on-demand (main survey §11).
  • Three time scales: seconds for failover and KV-state recovery, minutes for autoscaling and cold start, hours for tidal demand and spot or train-inference capacity scheduling. Slower recovery means more standing redundancy.
  • Liveput for preemption-prone deployments (expected throughput over the distribution of preemption scenarios), and prefill/decode-disaggregated pools treated as separate reliability units.
  • Evaluation hygiene: where the failures come from (production logs, replayed availability traces, or a killed process), whether the load is open-loop and bursty, and whether reported GPU·h savings subtract redundancy and spares (main survey §11 checklist).

3　Key technologies for 500k-NPU training

Ranked by the term of the budget each one moves (the reliability chapter's three levers: failure rate, recovery time, structure), and by what the 500k numbers above require.

  1. Failure domains that degrade instead of stopping the job. Superpod-scale domains with graceful loss of a plane or link: multi-plane fabrics with packet spraying (Meta's MRC keeps a 50k-GPU job running through 1–13 link flaps per minute, and one T0–T1 link failure costs 0.4 % of capacity), link-layer retrain and retransmission (Huawei claims over 99 % flap tolerance vendor), fault-tolerant collectives and NIC failover, and placement of DP replicas, checkpoints and spares aligned with rack, power-zone and cooling domains, since power events raise hardware failure probability 5–10× in LANL data. Huawei's 2026 claim that its optical engine cuts 48,000 modules to 5,500 and doubles fault-free running time is reported without a denominator web vendor.
  2. Detection and localization whose latency does not grow with N. In-band heartbeats and device plugins at 4–11 s (FlashRecovery), per-component probes at 2–60 s (ByteRobust), communication-layer triggers under 15 s regardless of scale (Mycroft), network skeleton probing at 8 s (SkeletonHunter), fail-slow detection in seconds (GREYHOUND), and grey-node eviction that lifted MTTF from 6.6 h to 16.7 h (Guard). Recovery time enters availability linearly while the failure rate enters the checkpoint term only as a square root, so this is where the leverage is.
  3. Local recovery instead of global restart. DP replicas as the fault unit (Meta's FT-HSDP at 98k GPUs: about 3 min to detect and handle plus about 100 s to rejoin), hot-swap and live migration (TrainMover's 11–21 s at 1k GPUs; Clockwork's live GPU migration web; PHOENIX), warm standby sized by a binomial P99 rather than a percentage (4 machines per 1,024 for ByteRobust), reduced-TP continuation for damaged replicas (NTP cuts throughput loss from 12 % to under 1 % with spare NVL domains), pipeline rerouting (ReCycle), and per-step fault tolerance without checkpoints (torchft, also on AMD with TorchTitan web). The order of the recovery ladder matters: predict, then network failover, replay, hot-swap, elastic, process restart, and global restart last. Restart initialization itself must be made scale-independent, since NCCL setup alone grew from 17 s to about 200 s between 16k and 98k GPUs.
  4. Loosely coupled training to shrink the synchronous domain. Decoupled DiLoCo (DeepMind, April 2026) splits the run into asynchronous islands so a failure stays local; its 1.2-million-chip event tape gives 88 % goodput against 58 % for elastic DP, with inter-DC bandwidth reduced by orders of magnitude. Real training was only at O(1k) TPUs and the training semantics change, so convergence must be re-validated per model.
  5. Near-zero exposed checkpoint cost and fast restore. In-memory and peer-replica checkpoints (GEMINI's 85 % to 97 % effective training time; Epoch's 0.32 s peer-memory save versus 2.4 s to storage), asynchronous hierarchical checkpointing (ByteCheckpoint, Check-N-Run), and restore read bandwidth accounted separately (about 5 min to read a 405B state). At 500k the optimal interval drops to minutes, so exposed save time must be seconds; HAC-D's no-fault bound is the quick feasibility screen.
  6. Silent corruption managed as its own budget. Episodes scale with N. The tools are online two-stage detection (AEGIS at 0.86–2.6 % overhead found 18 cases in 35 M GPU·h, all permanent and recurring on the same devices, so isolation follows detection), selective dual modular redundancy (SpareTrain at 3–14 % overhead), per-step detectors with last-step recompute (2026 injection studies web), device characterization showing that loss curves are not a sufficient monitor (LLM-PRISM), and retain-last-clean checkpoint semantics so that a late detection rolls back to a verified clean state.
  7. Spares and repair treated as a queue. Quantile-based spare sizing, sparing zones at the block size that balances stranding against repair (Meta's 8.6 / 4.7 / 2.6 % inter-block spares for 72 / 36 / 18-GPU blocks), repair turnaround of days (Unicron's 1–7 days), fault storms that saturate repair crews, and automated node replacement that cuts remediation from 60–120 min to 5–10 min. This is why HAC-D's first co-simulation target is the resource queue inside the ledger.
  8. Fleet hygiene and prediction. Acceptance screening and continuous sweeps, lemon-node eviction (Meta's large-job failures from 14 % to 4 %), repairing the components behind repeat network failures (SkeletonHunter's 99.1 % monthly reduction after fixing 98 % of 1,302 problem components), and failure prediction that turns interruptions into planned migrations at the next checkpoint.
  9. An observability and calibration loop. Event ledgers with root-cause deduplication, exposure hours and stage timestamps; continuous calibration; autonomous recovery engines (NVIDIA Mission Control is the productized form web). HAC-D's allocation result is the practical rule: at 500k hardware alone cannot reach the target, detection is the necessary mechanism, and once it is in place component rates may relax about 4× in the 50k worked example.

4　Where the field is thin

  • Validation against production. Most published reliability models are checked analytic-versus-simulation; AIReSim's validation is a single sentence, SPARe and Epoch have no real-machine comparison. HAC-D's confirmation layer (incident ledgers, injection campaigns, hold-out windows) exists to close this gap.
  • Common-cause parameters. No source publishes a β factor for power, cooling, switch or storage domains; LANL gives conditional amplification factors, hardware specifications give structure only.
  • Non-exponential and time-varying rates. Infant mortality (shape 0.7–0.8) and wear-out (shape about 5) point in opposite directions; checkpoint intervals and spare formulas still assume constant rates.
  • Elasticity at scale. No data on the failure rate of the elastic mechanisms themselves; Gemini 2.5's reported residual loss comes about half from the elastic mechanism's own tail. Degraded throughput is usually assumed proportional to online cards, which is optimistic.
  • Silent corruption rates. Field rates are reported in incompatible units (cumulative per-mille, per-device, per GPU·h); device-level injection gives relative rates only.
  • Cross-datacenter and 1 M-accelerator training. Models exist (DiLoCo scaling laws, cross-DC ring models) but validation is simulation or projection; the largest public end-to-end measurement is 2,048 H100s.

推理侧的专有部分

  • N+k 容量可用率而非"服务在线"：副本是 TP/EP 秩的串联，服务是带容量的副本 k-of-n，SLO 在窗口上评估。
  • 带故障切换与冷启动的请求级 DES。vLLM 183 s 的冷启动超过 2 分钟的 spot 抢占预警，这正是 SkyServe 超配 spot 副本并回退到按需实例的原因（主综述 §11）。
  • 三个时间尺度：秒级的故障切换与 KV 状态恢复，分钟级的扩缩容与冷启动，小时级的潮汐需求与 spot 或训推容量调度。恢复越慢，常备冗余越多。
  • Liveput：面向易被抢占部署的指标（抢占场景分布下的期望吞吐）；并把 prefill / decode 分离的资源池当作独立的可靠性单元。
  • 评估卫生：故障来自哪里（生产日志、回放的可用性 trace，还是杀掉一个进程），负载是否开环且带突发，报告的 GPU·h 节省是否扣除了冗余与备机（主综述 §11 清单）。

3　50 万卡 NPU 训练的关键技术

按每项技术所改变的预算项排序（可靠性章的三个杠杆：故障率、恢复时间、结构），并对照上面 50 万卡的数字。

  1. 会降级而不是让作业停下的故障域。超节点规模的故障域，丢一个平面或一条链路时优雅降级：多平面网络加逐包喷洒（Meta 的 MRC 让 5 万卡作业在每分钟 1–13 次链路闪断下继续运行，一条 T0–T1 链路故障只损失 0.4 % 容量），链路层重训练与重传（华为宣称闪断容忍超过 99 % vendor），容错集合通信与网卡切换，以及把 DP 副本、检查点与备件按机柜、供电区与制冷域放置——LANL 数据中电力事件让硬件故障概率升高 5–10 倍。华为 2026 年宣称其光引擎把 48,000 个光模块减到 5,500 个、无故障运行时间翻倍，该数字没有给出分母 web vendor。
  2. 时延不随 N 增长的检测与定位。带内心跳与 device plugin 4–11 s（FlashRecovery）、逐部件探针 2–60 s（ByteRobust）、与规模无关的通信层触发不到 15 s（Mycroft）、网络骨架探测 8 s（SkeletonHunter）、秒级的拖慢检测（GREYHOUND），以及把 MTTF 从 6.6 h 提到 16.7 h 的灰节点剔除（Guard）。恢复时间线性进入可用度，而故障率只以平方根进入检查点项，所以杠杆在这里。
  3. 以局部恢复替代全局重启。以 DP 副本为容错单元（Meta 的 FT-HSDP 在 9.8 万 GPU 上：约 3 分钟检测与处理、约 100 s 重新加入），热插拔与在线迁移（TrainMover 在 1 千卡上 11–21 s；Clockwork 的 GPU 在线迁移 web；PHOENIX），按二项分布 P99 而非百分比确定的温备（ByteRobust 每 1,024 台 4 台），受损副本降 TP 度继续运行（NTP 借助备用 NVL 域把吞吐损失从 12 % 压到 1 % 以下），流水线重路由（ReCycle），以及不依赖检查点的逐步容错（torchft，也已在 AMD 上与 TorchTitan 结合 web）。恢复阶梯的顺序很重要：先预测，再网络切换、重执行、热插拔、弹性、进程重启，最后才是全局重启。重启初始化本身也必须做到与规模无关：从 1.6 万到 9.8 万 GPU，仅 NCCL 初始化就从 17 s 增到约 200 s。
  4. 松耦合训练，缩小同步域。Decoupled DiLoCo（DeepMind，2026 年 4 月）把一次训练拆成异步的"岛"，让故障留在局部；其 120 万芯片的事件磁带给出 88 % 的 goodput，对比弹性 DP 的 58 %，跨数据中心带宽降低数个数量级。真实训练只做到 O(1k) 颗 TPU，而且训练语义随之改变，收敛性需要按模型重新验证。
  5. 接近零的暴露检查点开销与快速恢复读。内存级与对等副本检查点（GEMINI 把有效训练时间从 85 % 提到 97 %；Epoch 估算对等显存保存 0.32 s、存储 2.4 s），异步分级检查点（ByteCheckpoint、Check-N-Run），以及单独核算的恢复读带宽（读回一个 405B 状态约 5 分钟）。在 50 万卡最优间隔降到分钟级，所以暴露的保存时间必须压到秒级；HAC-D 的无故障上界是快速的可行性筛查。
  6. 把静默错误当作独立预算管理。发生次数随 N 增长。工具有：在线两阶段检测（AEGIS 以 0.86–2.6 % 的开销在 3,500 万 GPU·h 中发现 18 例，全部为持续性故障且在同一设备上复发，因此检出之后要隔离），选择性双模冗余（SpareTrain，开销 3–14 %），带末步重算的逐步检测器（2026 年的注入研究 web），说明 loss 曲线不足以作为监控的器件刻画（LLM-PRISM），以及"保留最后干净点"的检查点语义，使晚发现的错误能回退到已验证的干净状态。
  7. 把备件与维修当作队列。按分位数确定备件数，把备件区划在平衡 stranding 与维修的块大小上（Meta 对 72 / 36 / 18 卡块给出 8.6 / 4.7 / 2.6 % 的块间备件），以天计的维修周转（Unicron 的 1–7 天），会打满维修人力的故障风暴，以及把处置时间从 60–120 分钟压到 5–10 分钟的自动换机。这正是 HAC-D 把账本内的资源队列作为首个联合仿真对象的原因。
  8. 机队卫生与预测。验收筛查与持续巡检，柠檬节点剔除（Meta 的大作业故障从 14 % 降到 4 %），修复反复引发网络故障的部件（SkeletonHunter 修复 1,302 个问题部件中的 98 % 后，月故障率下降 99.1 %），以及把中断变成下一个检查点处计划迁移的故障预测。
  9. 可观测与标定闭环。按根因去重、带暴露小时与各阶段时间戳的事件台账；持续标定；自主恢复引擎（NVIDIA Mission Control 是其产品化形态 web）。HAC-D 的分配结果给出实用规则：在 50 万卡仅靠硬件无法达标，检测是必要机制；检测到位之后，5 万卡算例中部件故障率可以放宽约 4 倍。

4　领域的薄弱之处

  • 对生产数据的确认。多数已发表的可靠性模型只做解析对仿真的检查；AIReSim 的验证只有一句话，SPARe 与 Epoch 没有真机对照。HAC-D 的确认层（事件台账、注入实验、留出窗口）正是为补这一缺口而设。
  • 共因参数。没有任何来源公开供电、制冷、交换机或存储域的 β 因子；LANL 给的是条件放大倍数，硬件规格只给结构。
  • 非指数与时变故障率。婴儿期（形状 0.7–0.8）与耗损（形状约 5）指向相反方向；检查点间隔与备件公式仍假设恒定速率。
  • 规模化的弹性。没有弹性机制自身失效率的数据；Gemini 2.5 报告的残余损失约一半来自弹性机制自身的尾部。降级吞吐通常按在线卡数线性假设，偏乐观。
  • 静默错误率。现场率以不兼容的单位报告（累计万分比、每器件、每 GPU·h）；器件级注入只给相对率。
  • 跨数据中心与百万加速器训练。模型存在（DiLoCo scaling law、跨 DC 环模型），但验证是仿真或投影；公开的最大端到端实测是 2,048 张 H100。
