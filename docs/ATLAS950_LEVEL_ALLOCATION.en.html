Atlas 950: reliability mechanisms mapped to topology levels, and the hardware and mechanism allocation at each level

Scope as requested: failure rates only from the 54-event catalog of ReMAP / ai-cluster-reliability (business-impact FIT per card, NPU-equivalent; no HAC-D per-unit conversion, no scale-out analogue classes); mechanisms only ReMAP’s reference set (auditReferenceMechanisms = D-SDC, R-SDC, R-REPLAY, R-PROCESS, R-ELASTIC, C-FAST at ReMAP’s policy parameters) on ReMAP’s baseline recovery (detection 95 % in 10 s, the rest 60 s; R-CKPT automatic 180 s at 99 %; R-MANUAL 2 h); allocation methods all HAC-D’s (pause budget B, ARINC / AGREE / minimum-effort splits, the recovery factor, the v4 optimal strategy with recovery levers, derated check, window-ledger verification, Shapley). The Atlas 950 SuperPoD (1,024 NPUs, 64 per cabinet, 8 per node) gives the levels and the instances per level. Target: A_op ≥ 98.5 % for one synchronous training job over a 30-day window, nominally and with every mechanism derated (coverage −5 pt, success −8 pt, times ×1.5); main case 500k.

  • It maps: the 54 events of the ReMAP catalog fall on the five Atlas 950 levels by their own scope — NPU (1,577 FIT per card), node (633), compute cabinet / Pod (447), SuperPoD (133), cluster (543), 3,333 FIT per card in total as in the repositories; the six reference mechanisms sit where they are deployed: R-REPLAY, D-SDC and R-SDC at the NPU level, R-PROCESS at the node level, R-ELASTIC, C-FAST and the baseline recovery (detection, R-CKPT, R-MANUAL, isolation) at the cluster level.
  • Where a mechanism acts is not where it sits: the cluster-level R-ELASTIC removes NPU- and node-level downtime (95 + 50 s/h at 500k); R-PROCESS and R-REPLAY also act only on NPU and node events (and the SuperPoD optics). The cluster and cabinet levels carry 84 % of today’s downtime and no reference mechanism has a fast rung for them — only the baseline detection and global recovery, or hardware.
  • 500k, A_op ≥ 98.5 % (nominal and derated): today 95.66 % / 95.18 %; the budget is 46.1 s of pause per hour (today 173.7 s). ① Hardware alone: every class’s FIT × 0.265; ② recovery chain alone: detection, automatic attempt and manual times all × 0.20 (automatic attempt 36 s); ③ least effort: automatic attempt 180 → 60 s, manual 2 h → 30 min, then ARINC hardware × 0.504 — 99.06 % nominal, exactly 98.50 % derated, all 400 30-day windows meet it.
  • Allocation at each level (plan ③, Tables 3–5): SuperPoD-level hardware ≤ 68,834 FIT per SuperPoD (MTBF ≥ 1.7 y), ≤ 14,412 FIT per compute cabinet (7.9 y), ≤ 2,554 FIT per node, ≤ 795 FIT per NPU; on the mechanism side the six reference mechanisms keep ReMAP’s parameters (still meeting the target derated) and the cluster-level automatic attempt is 60 s, manual 30 min. A minimum-effort split puts the hardware task on the cluster level (UNKNOWN, STORAGE) and the cabinet level (POWER, MGMT, COOL).
  • Other sizes: 10k and 100k already meet it (hardware slack ×15.7 and ×1.53: the allowed values are ceilings, not tasks); 200k only needs the automatic attempt at 60 s (98.57 % derated, 90.5 % of windows, verdict uncertain). 口径: A_op = ReMAP’s “operational availability”; FIT on ReMAP’s per-card basis, per-instance values = FIT per card × cards in the instance, exposure counted once.

  • Why by level: synchronous training is a series system — a hard interruption at any level stops the whole job; the levels matter for “who delivers”: NPU, node, compute cabinet and SuperPoD each map to a supply / acceptance object (Table 5), the cluster level to the job-level services and operations. Level budgets are design references (as in ReMAP’s layered diagnosis); the total target is closed on HAC-D’s engine as a whole, no level must meet its share on its own.
  • Limits of the reference mechanisms: all six provide fast rungs only at the NPU and node levels; cabinet- and cluster-level downtime can only be lowered by the baseline detection, R-CKPT, R-MANUAL or hardware. Mechanisms for those levels (e.g. control-plane continuity, fault classification, highly available storage) need ReMAP’s extensions or HAC-D’s library and are outside this page.
  • Uncertainty: the catalog FIT is an analogue prior from GPU fleets (per card), not an Atlas 950 measurement; mechanism parameters are ReMAP’s nominal targets; levels follow the catalog scope, FABRIC / OPT on the SuperPoD level is a judgement for the 950 (the in-cabinet LRS are included). The shape of the result — cluster and cabinet levels dominate, the reference mechanisms do not reach them, recovery levers first relax the hardware task from ×0.27 to ×0.50 — does not hinge on these assumptions.
