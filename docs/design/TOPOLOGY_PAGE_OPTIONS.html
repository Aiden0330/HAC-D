HAC-D · design proposal · 2026-10-16

New first page: topology → hardware → fault types → mechanisms — three frontend options

Each wireframe shows the same state: CloudMatrix384 template at 10,000 NPUs with the six default mechanisms. Counts come from HAC-D's computeCounts (CloudMatrix384 preset, full-pod fill); KPIs from the 54-event reference catalog with the six default mechanisms; FIT per unit derived from the catalog under the CloudMatrix384 geometry. In every option the Training page is merged into this page and the Audit page disappears from the navigation (its audit moves to Mechanisms, its cross-scale study to Allocation). Recommendation: C, with the three topology cards of A as the template selector at the top of the input pane. It keeps every current editor, merges Training without losing anything, and its hardware tab shows the four standard scales side by side.

A · Picture-first canvas

Layout
three topology cards and the scale chips on top; a large live picture with the hardware table beside it; KPI strip and the six default mechanisms underneath; the former Training page collapsed at the bottom.
Merges
Scenario and Training. Mechanisms and Allocation stay separate pages.
Best for
presenting and comparing architectures; reviews with people who think in pictures.
Cost and risk
medium effort (three parameterised SVG generators). Detailed editing (components, recovery stages, JSON) moves below the fold.
Phone
cards and table stack vertically; the picture scrolls sideways.

B · Guided stepper

Layout
a vertical stepper (topology, hardware, fault types, mechanisms, constraints and strategy, results) with one panel per step and a running KPI bar.
Merges
Scenario, Training, Mechanisms and Allocation into one guided flow; the separate pages remain for experts.
Best for
a fixed workflow, new users, demonstrations.
Cost and risk
highest effort (new navigation model and per-step state). Experts have to click through steps to change one value.
Phone
the steps become an accordion.

C · Split workbench

Layout
inputs on the left in collapsible groups (template, scale, hardware parameters, fault library, mechanisms, workload, recovery, JSON); outputs on the right as tabs (picture, hardware, reliability, allocation) under a KPI strip. The hardware tab shows 1k / 10k / 20k / 50k side by side with the current N highlighted.
Merges
Scenario and Training; Allocation reachable as a tab.
Best for
daily modelling work: every edit recomputes in place, nothing is more than one click away.
Cost and risk
lowest effort (reuses today’s editors and Training cards). Dense; the picture is one tab among four.
Phone
the panes stack; the tabs stay.
