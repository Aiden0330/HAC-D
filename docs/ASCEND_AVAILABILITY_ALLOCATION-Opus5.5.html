1. 分配方法

两种对照分配：① 只靠硬件——恢复不变，所有中断类 FIT 乘同一系数（ARINC；AGREE 与最小努力是另两种拆分）；② 只靠恢复链——硬件不变，检测、自动尝试、人工三个时间同乘 g（HAC-D 恢复系数）。推荐分配：HAC-D 最优策略在三个恢复杠杆的全部组合上取“杠杆努力 + 硬件努力”最小（硬件按 ARINC 补齐），再做逐窗口校核；硬件有余量时，允许系数 k 取同时满足降额均值与逐窗口校核的最大值。目标若是有效产出（ReMAP 的主指标），还需检查点与静默检出机制，本页不展开。

Allocating the availability goal on Ascend clusters: two Atlas 950 topologies and the 910C, 10k–500k NPUs

Goal: A_op ≥ 98.5 % (ReMAP’s “operational availability”) for one synchronous training job over a 30-day window, met nominally, with every mechanism derated and window by window. Data: the 54-event catalog of ReMAP / ai-cluster-reliability, per card (hard 3,333.3, sub-health 69.0, silent 48.3 FIT per card). Mechanisms: only ReMAP’s six reference mechanisms (D-SDC, R-SDC, R-REPLAY, R-PROCESS, R-ELASTIC, C-FAST) on the baseline recovery (detection 95 % in 10 s, the rest 60 s; R-CKPT automatic 180 s at 99 %; R-MANUAL 2 h). Method: HAC-D (Section 1). Topologies: the three below; per-card event rates do not depend on the topology, so the three share availability and per-card budgets, and the topology sets the size of each layer’s instances and with it the acceptance values per instance (Section 5).

1. Allocation method

Two reference allocations: ① hardware alone — recovery unchanged, every interruption class’s FIT times one factor (ARINC; AGREE and minimum effort are the other splits); ② recovery chain alone — hardware unchanged, detection, automatic-attempt and manual times all × g (HAC-D recovery factor). The recommended allocation: HAC-D’s optimal strategy takes the least lever + hardware effort over every subset of the three recovery levers (hardware closed by ARINC), then the window check; where the hardware has headroom, the allowed factor k is the largest that keeps both the derated mean and the window check. A goodput goal (ReMAP’s headline metric) also needs the checkpoint and silent-corruption mechanisms and is not covered here.

  • How to use it: take the plan for the size from Table 1; set the mechanism and recovery-process parameters from Table 3 (nominal values are requirements and must still hold derated); give suppliers and integrators their acceptance values per card, node, cabinet and SuperPoD from Tables 4 and 5; after deployment write the measured FIT and recovery times back and rerun.
  • Limits: the FIT values are an analogue prior from GPU fleets (per card), not 910C / 950 measurements; mechanism parameters are ReMAP’s nominal targets; FABRIC / OPT on the SuperPoD layer is a judgement for the UB domain; the goal is A_op, not goodput — the recommended designs deliver about 92 % goodput at 200k and 86 % at 500k.
